Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

In short

Jugnu keeps your reminders on your phone. If you sign in to use Jugnu AI, we store your name, email and plan, and send the photos or text you give Jugnu AI to an AI service to read them. We don't sell your data or show ads.

Data that stays on your phone

Your routines, reminders, medicines, alarm times, history and settings are stored only on your device. They are not uploaded. Uninstalling Jugnu deletes them.

If you sign in (Jugnu AI)

Signing in with Google is optional and only needed for Jugnu AI. When you sign in we store:

  • your name, email address and profile picture link from your Google account;
  • your plan (free trial or Jugnu Pro), its end date, and any payment you made to us by UPI or cash (amount, method, date);
  • a record of each Jugnu AI use: which feature, when, and whether it worked. The content you sent is not kept in this record.

This is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase.

Prescription photos and typed reminders

When you use Jugnu AI, the photo or text is sent over an encrypted connection to an AI service (Anthropic's Claude, or Google's Gemini as a backup) to read it, and the result is sent back to your phone. Jugnu does not store the photo or the text on its servers. The AI providers handle the request under their own API terms. What Jugnu AI reads is saved only on your phone, and only after you check it and tap Save.

Permissions Jugnu asks for

  • Notifications, alarms, full-screen alarms, turn screen on, display over other apps, run in background: so reminders ring on time and show over the lock screen.
  • Camera and photos: only when you choose to scan a prescription.
  • Internet: only for sign-in and Jugnu AI.

Sharing

We don't sell or rent your data and don't use it for advertising. It is shared only with the services that run Jugnu (Supabase for accounts, Google for sign-in, Anthropic and Google for Jugnu AI), or if the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Use Jugnu without an account: all alarm features work.
  • Sign out at any time in Settings.
  • Ask us to delete your account and its records by emailing 7066.mayu@gmail.com. We do this within 30 days.

Health information

Jugnu is a reminder tool, not a medical device, and does not give medical advice. Check everything Jugnu AI reads against your prescription and follow your doctor.

Children

Jugnu accounts are not meant for children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we'll update this page and its date. Questions: 7066.mayu@gmail.com
