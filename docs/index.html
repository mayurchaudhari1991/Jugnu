What Jugnu does

Jugnu is an Android app for anything you must not forget: medicines, workouts, water, bills, birthdays. Each reminder rings like an alarm clock, covers the lock screen, and waits until you tap Done, Snooze or Skip.

  • Routines ring several items one after another with a gap you choose, like 7 medicines 10 minutes apart.
  • Reminders repeat daily, on chosen weekdays, monthly, yearly or once.
  • Today and History show what's done, snoozed or missed.
  • Any snooze length, any alarm sound, vibration patterns or vibrate-only.

Jugnu AI (optional)

Signed-in users can photograph a doctor's prescription and Jugnu lists each medicine with its times, ready to check and save. You can also type a reminder in plain words ("Pay rent on the 5th at 10 am"). New accounts get a free trial; Jugnu Pro is available after that.

Jugnu AI can misread handwriting. Always check what it reads and follow your doctor's advice.

Your data

Your routines, reminders and history stay on your phone. An account is needed only for Jugnu AI. Read the privacy policy.
