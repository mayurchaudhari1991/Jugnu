Built so a reminder can't slip by.

Most reminder apps send a notification you swipe away. Jugnu rings, fills the screen and waits for an answer, then gets the next thing ready.

A real alarm, even on a locked phone.

When it's time, Jugnu lights up the screen, plays a sound and vibrates, the way an alarm clock does. You answer it without unlocking.

  • Done, Snooze or Skip right on the alarm. Skipped and missed ones are kept in History.
  • Rings again if you don't answer: up to 5 times, then it's marked missed and the routine carries on.
  • Snooze for any length: tap a quick choice or type any number of minutes.
  • Survives restarts: alarms come back after the phone reboots, and ring at the exact minute.

Your whole day on one screen.

Today shows what's done and what's next. Tick things off, or take one early with a single tap.

  • Up next card with a countdown and Done now / Snooze buttons.
  • Progress at a glance: "4 of 6 done today", coloured by routine.
  • Change your mind: tap a tick again to undo it.

Routines that ring one after another.

Put several things in a routine, set a start time and a gap. Jugnu rings them in order. Single reminders work too.

  • The gap is always kept: answer late or snooze, and the rest move with you, so two medicines never ring too close.
  • Its own time for any item that shouldn't follow the chain, like a hair oil at 11 PM.
  • Repeat the way life does: every day, chosen weekdays, a day each month, once a year, or just once.
  • Reorder, rename, recolour: every item has its own icon, colour, dose and note shown on the alarm.

Make it sound and feel like yours.

Pick how Jugnu rings for everything, or give one routine its own sound.

  • Any sound: your phone's alarm tones, or a song from your files.
  • Vibration patterns: Pulse, Heartbeat, Long buzz, Gentle, or off.
  • Vibrate only: for meetings, the library or a sleeping baby.
  • Gentle start: the sound rises over a few seconds instead of blasting.

You stay in charge.

Nothing is saved until you check it. Jugnu points out anything it couldn't read clearly.

  • Up to 4 pages from the camera or your gallery, read in about 10–30 seconds.
  • Edit before saving: name, dose, morning / afternoon / evening / night, before or after food, notes.
  • Joins your routines: each medicine slots into Morning, Afternoon, Evening or Night with the right gap.
  • Or just type it: "Gym every Mon, Wed and Fri at 6:30 pm", or "Kal subah 7 baje Papa ko call karna".

Jugnu AI can misread handwriting. Always check the list against your prescription and follow your doctor.

Questions

Why does Jugnu ask for so many permissions?

Each one helps an alarm get through: notifications to ring at all, exact alarms for the right minute, full-screen and "turn screen on" to show over the lock screen, and battery settings so the phone doesn't put Jugnu to sleep. Jugnu explains each step on its first screen.

Is it safe to install from outside the Play Store?

Yes. The file is signed by Jugnu, and Android checks that signature on every update. Play Protect warns about any app installed from a file; tap Install anyway.

My alarm didn't show over the lock screen. What now?

Some phones (OnePlus, Oppo, Xiaomi, Vivo) switch off a background setting. Open Jugnu, then Settings, and follow the row that isn't ticked. Jugnu also shows a warning on Today when this happens.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything except Jugnu AI works without one. Sign in with Google only when you want to scan prescriptions or type reminders.

Is Jugnu medical advice?

No. Jugnu is a reminder tool. Always check what Jugnu AI reads against your prescription and follow your doctor.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. Jugnu is Android only for now.
